FAQ

Straight answers before you book

The questions people ask most before they hop on a call.

Do you charge a fee for consulting?

No. Plan guidance and comparisons don’t come with an invoice to you. Like most independent agents, compensation comes from carriers when you enroll. Your job is to get clear options, not pay for a pitch.

What happens on the free 15‑minute call?

You tell Chase your situation (state, household, budget, doctors, timeline). He outlines next steps and whether he can help. No pressure to buy on the call.

Can you help small businesses with only 2-3 people?

Yes. Micro-groups and owner-only setups are common. Chase can walk through group vs individual paths and what usually fits a smaller team budget.

Why use an agent instead of going straight to a carrier?

One carrier only shows their own products. An independent agent can compare across options for your state so you’re not stuck in one company’s catalog.

Do I have to wait for open enrollment?

No. Private insurance can be purchased any time of year — you don’t have to wait for open enrollment. Marketplace plans and some other pathways still have open-enrollment windows (or special enrollment after a life event), but Chase can walk you through private options that start when you need them. Book a free call and we’ll map what fits your situation.

What states do you serve?

I can help in most states — everywhere except California, Connecticut, Washington, Oregon, New Mexico, New York, New Jersey, New Hampshire, Massachusetts, Maine, Minnesota, North Dakota, Idaho, Hawaii, Rhode Island, and Alaska.
