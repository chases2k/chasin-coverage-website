Privacy Policy

Chasin' Coverage · Effective October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Chasin' Coverage (Chase Tabor, independent licensed insurance agent) collects through chasincoverage.com and our TikTok integration, and what we do with it.

1. Information you give us

If you request a consultation through our website form, Calendly, text, email, or social media, we receive what you choose to share: typically your name, contact details, state, who needs coverage, and your current insurance situation. We use it only to schedule and prepare for your consultation and to follow up about it.

2. Information we do not ask for online

We do not ask for Social Security numbers, dates of birth, health conditions, or payment information through our website, social media, or automated assistants. That information is only collected, if needed, during a consultation or through a carrier's own secure application.

3. The TikTok integration

Our TikTok app is used solely by us to publish our own videos to our own TikTok account (@chasincoverage). It does not access, store, or process information about any other TikTok user. Interactions with our TikTok content (views, comments, direct messages) are governed by TikTok's own privacy policy.

4. Automated assistants

Our website chat and some message replies may be handled by an automated assistant. It will identify itself as an AI if asked. It does not collect sensitive personal information and will direct you to a consultation with Chase for anything plan-specific.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with insurance carriers when you ask us to apply for coverage, with service providers that help us operate (for example, scheduling and email tools), and when required by law.

6. Cookies and analytics

chasincoverage.com may use basic analytics and embedded third-party tools (such as Calendly) that set their own cookies. We do not use advertising trackers on the site.

7. Retention and your choices

We keep consultation information as long as needed to serve you and as required by insurance record-keeping rules. You can ask us to update or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, at any time using the contact details below. We only send text messages to people who have messaged us first or asked to be contacted.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
