Privacy Policy

Chasin' Coverage · Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information Chasin' Coverage (Robert "Chase" Tabor, independent licensed insurance agent, doing business as Chasin' Coverage) collects through chasincoverage.com, our text-messaging line, and our TikTok integration, and what we do with it.

1. Information you give us

If you request a consultation through our website form, Calendly, text message, phone call, email, or social media, we receive what you choose to share: typically your name, phone number, email address, state or ZIP code, who needs coverage, and your current insurance situation. We use it only to respond to your request, schedule and prepare for your consultation, and follow up about it.

2. Information we do not ask for online

We do not ask for Social Security numbers, dates of birth, health conditions, or payment information through our website, text messages, social media, or automated assistants. That information is only collected, if needed, during a consultation or through a carrier's own secure application.

3. The TikTok integration

Our TikTok app is used solely by us to publish our own videos to our own TikTok account (@chasincoverage). It does not access, store, or process information about any other TikTok user. Interactions with our TikTok content (views, comments, direct messages) are governed by TikTok's own privacy policy.

4. Automated assistants

Our website chat, phone line, and text-message replies may be handled by an automated AI assistant. It identifies itself as an AI assistant at the start of a conversation and whenever asked. It does not collect sensitive personal information and will direct you to a consultation with a licensed agent for anything plan-specific.

5. Text messaging (SMS)

We only send text messages to people who have texted us first, asked to be texted during a phone call, or opted in through our website form. If you text us or opt in, we use your mobile phone number only to reply to your request, send quotes and appointment details and reminders, and follow up on your inquiry. Mobile phone numbers and text-messaging opt-in consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Message frequency varies based on your conversation. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or reply HELP for help.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with insurance carriers when you ask us to apply for coverage, with service providers that help us operate (for example, scheduling, messaging, and email tools, which may process your information only on our behalf), and when required by law.

7. Cookies and analytics

chasincoverage.com may use basic analytics and embedded third-party tools (such as Calendly) that set their own cookies. We do not use advertising trackers on the site.

8. Retention and your choices

We keep consultation information as long as needed to serve you and as required by insurance record-keeping rules. You can ask us to update or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, at any time using the contact details below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
